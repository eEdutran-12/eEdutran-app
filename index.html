Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book a free trial session? ▼
You can easily book your free trial session by clicking any "Book Your Free Session Now" button across our website. Simply fill in your details, and our team will get in touch with you immediately to schedule a convenient time slot!
What happens after the trial session? Is there any obligation? ▼
Everything after the trial will be directly communicated over on WhatsApp for your convenience. There are absolute zero obligations if you don't like it—feel completely free to walk away with no pressure or hidden catches!
How do you conduct online tutoring sessions? ▼
Our online tutoring sessions are hosted via Google Meet paired with advanced interactive virtual whiteboards. Students can write, draw, view diagrams live, and access all past session notes anytime for seamless collaborative learning.
What subjects and levels do you teach? (Detailed Overview) ▼
We provide expert, tailored tutoring across multiple foundational and advanced academic tiers:

• KS2 Level (Primary): Comprehensive building blocks covering Math, English, Science, and Coding.
• KS3 Level (Lower Secondary): Deep dives into English, Math, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Geography, History, and Coding.
• GCSE Level (Core Exams): Intensive exam-focused preparation for Math, Further Math, English, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and ICT / Computer Science to secure top grades.
• A-Level (Advanced Study): Rigorous mentoring for complex modules in Math, Further Math, Statistics, Mechanics, English, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Computer Science to conquer university entry requirements.
Are you an agency or a tutoring company? ▼
We are not any agency! We are a dedicated group of professional tutors. Parents get to contact and communicate with the tutors directly with complete transparency.
What are your pricing and subscription charges? ▼
Our standard charges are just £14 per hour, plus a mere £1 monthly fee for the number of subjects chosen. There are absolute zero hidden charges!
What qualifications and experience do your tutors have? ▼
All our tutors possess a minimum of 10+ years of teaching experience, bringing deep academic expertise and proven student success results to every session.
Can students access notes or pages from past sessions? ▼
Yes! All pages tutored during sessions are safely stored and can be accessed anytime by students for revision or completing homework assignments.
